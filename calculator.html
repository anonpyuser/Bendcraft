About this calculator

Bendcraft’s original trade calculator is inspired by the mixed-unit workflow of Workman’s Calculator Pro. Length × quantity returns length. Length × width returns area. Length ÷ length returns a unitless ratio. Equal spacing uses positions at both endpoints.
