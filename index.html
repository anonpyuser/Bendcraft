Field guide & iPhone setup

Tap a measurement field then use the built-in number and fraction buttons—no iPhone keyboard needed. Fractions such as 12 3/8 work. Values use the selected unit. Change units to convert your current measurements. Angles remain degrees.

Offsets and saddles use ideal centerline geometry: spacing = height ÷ sin(angle); shrink per offset = height × tan(angle ÷ 2). These differ from rounded field tables and do not compensate for shoe radius. Verify marks with your bender’s instructions. Stub take-up and back-to-back star marks must match your actual shoe. Segmented calculations use equal chord approximations.

On iPhone: open the hosted app in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App. Saved bends stay in this browser on this device. This local version requires hosting before iPhone installation.
